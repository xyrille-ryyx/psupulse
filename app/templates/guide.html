{{ card_title('calendar', 'The weekly cycle') }}
  1. Each week, a feedback window opens for students.
  2. Students send short, anonymous comments about their courses.
  3. When the window closes, each comment's sentiment (positive, negative or neutral) and topic are found automatically by text analysis.
  4. Results are combined by week, course, department and college, and a weekly report is generated.
  5. Alerts flag sharp rises in negative comments, recurring concerns and safety issues, and staff record what was done.
