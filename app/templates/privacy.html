Privacy notice

PSU Pulse follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

Your comment, the course you're commenting on, and the date you sent it. We do not save your name or student number with your comment.

How we keep you anonymous

  • Your comment is saved without your name or ID. To stop duplicate submissions, we keep a scrambled one-way code that can't be turned back into your identity.
  • Personal details you type (names after "my name is", phone numbers, emails, ID numbers, links) are removed automatically before anyone reads the comment.
  • Results for a class are shown only when at least the minimum number of students have commented, so no single person can be singled out.
  • Only authorized staff can see results, and only for the units they handle. Every report view and download is logged.

How your feedback is used

Comments are grouped by week, course and topic, and their sentiment (positive, negative or neutral) is measured automatically. Staff use the weekly reports to act on recurring concerns.

Questions

Contact the Student Affairs office or the university Data Protection Officer.
